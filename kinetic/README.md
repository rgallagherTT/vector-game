# KINETIC

Third-person telekinesis action in a rain-soaked neon city. One self-contained `index.html` — Three.js + Rapier physics from CDN, all art procedural.

- Grab and throw anything (cars, buses, train cars, officers, armour plates), push, slam, dodge, telekinetic jump.
- Heat 1–5: patrols, roadblocks, tactical squads and armoured trucks, helicopters with searchlights, rooftop snipers, drones, containment teams, dampener drones, and the Warden — a four-legged walker boss in three phases that comes back stronger.
- Advanced powers: debris shield, Catch, levitate, heavy lift, vortex, overload — unlocked with kinesis in the power tree.
- Four districts: Neon Row, the Harbour, the Financial Core, the Underworks (subway between districts).
- Challenges, daily run, records, cosmetics, photo mode (`P`), end-of-run highlight replay.

Press `H` in game for controls. `?debug=1` for the debug overlay and keys; `?bench=chaos` / `?bench=warden` for benchmarks.
