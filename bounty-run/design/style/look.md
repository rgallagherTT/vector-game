# BOUNTY RUN — look notes

## Light by time of day
- **Noon**: sun #fff4dc, 70° high, intensity 3.5. Hard, short shadows; sand nearly white in the highlights. Heat haze strongest (distortion 0.006 UV, rising).
- **Golden hour**: sun #ffb050 at 12°, intensity 3. Long shadows across dunes (shadow length 4–5× object height). Every bike gets a warm rim; this is the hero look.
- **Sunset**: sky gradient #ff7a2a at the horizon to #7a4aa0 overhead. Sun almost horizontal; the shadow side of dunes goes violet, not black.
- **Night**: moon and sky fill #1a2a4a, cold and blue; huge star field #cfe0ff. Thruster glow, hover pads and headlights become the main lights — let them light the sand under the bike.

## Dust and haze
- Distance haze #e8cfa0 by day: FogExp2 density 0.0025; mesas fade to a flat warm silhouette by 400 m.
- Every bike throws a sand rooster-tail from its rear pads and thrusters: 3–6 m long at boost, lit by the sun from behind (back-scatter brightens it).
- Dust hangs: plumes linger 2–4 s and drift with the wind. Chases leave visible trails across the map.
- Sandstorms: visibility to 30 m, everything shifts to a single ochre tone, lights bloom heavily.

## Materials
- Paint is sand-scoured: brighter speckle on exposed faces, rust in seams, welded patch plates. Chrome is the only clean surface — Chrome Saints keep it that way.
- Emissives (mat_emissive_*): thrusters intensity 6, hover pads 3.5, headlights 8. Flicker them when the part is damaged.
